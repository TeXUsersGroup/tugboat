Title: A Brazilian Portuguese work on MetaPost, and how mathematics is embedded in it

Summary: summary of thesis on the mathematics of MetaPost, with graphical examples.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Estevão Vinícius Candia

Publication: TUGboat volume 40, number 3 (2019), pages 247–250

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/40-3/tb126candia-mpost
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Graphics

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: This article presents a summary of my final work [1] for the master’s degree course in Mathematics, presented on November 1, 2018. This work shows an introduction to the basic commands of the MetaPost language. For this, some mathematical concepts are explained, as they are part of its programming. It is shown how these concepts can be used to create high-quality figures used in areas of mathematics such as geometry and the study of graphs of real functions.
