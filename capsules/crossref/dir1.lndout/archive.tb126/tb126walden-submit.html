Title: An experience of trying to submit a paper in LaTeX in an XML-first world

Summary: a report on a LaTeX submission to IEEE Annals, and consequent expectations.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: David Walden

Publication: TUGboat volume 40, number 3 (2019), pages 215–216

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/40-3/tb126walden-submit
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Publishing

Difficulty: Introductory

Abstract: The publishing world is increasingly using an XML-first work flow wherein the source manuscript is first converted to XML, editing is likely done in XML, and output formats are created from what comes out of XML. [1] This note describes some recent author experience with a publisher that was moving in that direction.

References:

  1. Jonathan McGlone, Preserving and Publishing Digital Content Using XML Workflows, tinyurl.com/xml-mcglone
