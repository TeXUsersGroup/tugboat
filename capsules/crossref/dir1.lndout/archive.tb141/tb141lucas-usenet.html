Title: Usenet and the future of comp.text.tex

Summary: Usenet and c.t.t history and access methods without Google.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Authors: Rayner Lucas, Tristan Miller, Marco Moock

Publication: TUGboat volume 45, number 3 (2024), pages 298–301

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/45-3/tb141lucas-usenet
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Resources

Difficulty: Introductory

Abstract: Usenet has served as a venue for announcements and discussions on the TeX ecosystem for over 40 years, and remains in operation despite the recent shuttering of the Web-to-news gateway on Google Groups. This article (re)introduces readers to Usenet and its comp.text.tex newsgroup, discusses the implications of their severance from Google, and explains how users can continue to access them.
