Title: My website exploring language support and more in libre TrueType and OpenType fonts: typosetting.co.uk

Summary: extensive listing of free fonts, including available styles, small caps, CJK information, etc.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Ken Moffat

Publication: TUGboat volume 45, number 3 (2024), pages 324–327

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/45-3/tb141moffat-typosetting
(previous doi - next doi)

Categories: Fonts - Resources

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: At typosetting.co.uk I have a website where I explore libre OpenType and TrueType fonts to try to see which languages they support (and to note mismapping of codepoints when I spot them). I am using XeLaTeX to produce PDFs showing the glyphs in the fonts, the languages I think they support, and some other comparisons between fonts.
