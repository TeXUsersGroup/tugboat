Title: The LaTeX release workflow and the LaTeX dev formats

Summary: regression testing, user testing, and new pdflatex-dev etc. formats.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Frank Mittelbach (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 40, number 2 (2019), pages 153–156

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/40-2/tb125mitt-dev

Category: LaTeX

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: How do you prevent creating banana software (i.e., software that gets ripe at the customer site)? By proper testing! But this is anything but easy.

The paper will give an overview of the efforts made by the LaTeX Project Team over the years to provide high-quality software and explains the changes that we have made this summer to improve the situation further.
