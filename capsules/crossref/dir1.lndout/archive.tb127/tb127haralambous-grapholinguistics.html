Title: Grapholinguistics, TeX, and a June 2020 conference

Summary: background of subject, annotated topic list, relationship to TeX.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Yannis Haralambous

Publication: TUGboat volume 41, number 1 (2020), pages 12–19

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/41-1/tb127haralambous-grapholinguistics
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Humanities

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: This paper presents the conference Grapholinguistics in the 21st Century that was to take place in Paris, in June 2020. With the global health situation, it will now be held as a video conference (grafematik2020.sciencesconf.org). We give an introduction to the discipline of grapholinguistics, the history and the topics of the conference, and we close with the fundamental question: why should a TeX user join the conference?

Originally written for MAPS 50 (2020). Reprinted with permission.
