Title: Your personal LaTeX bookshelf: Improving your background in a time of lockdown

Summary: randomized sizes and colors for book spines from BibTeX sources.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Peter Flynn

Publication: TUGboat volume 41, number 2 (2020), pages 157–159

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/41-2/tb128flynn-bookshelf
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Graphics

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: This paper describes the development of a LaTeX package to create a bookshelf image from a BibTeX file, suitable for use as a background for a video call in Zoom, Skype, or similar. Each entry is typeset as the spine of a book with title and author, using a randomly-selected font, color, and size. The paper describes the problems of random choice with both fixed-length and [potentially] endless lists, and the algorithm used to fit the author and title onto the spine. The package is available as bookshelf on CTAN for inspection and testing.
