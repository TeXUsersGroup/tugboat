Abstract: The coronavirus pandemic threw a monkey wrench in the plans to hold TUG2020 at the Rochester Institute of Technology. So a decision to hold it online was inevitable, if the conference was to be held at all. With the help of many people, everything came together and an exciting program was presented with remarkably few glitches.
