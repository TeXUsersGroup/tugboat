Title: The HINT Project: Status and open questions

Summary: mobile and tablet reading of TeX output; soliciting information for future work.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Authors: Martin Ruckert, Gudrun Socher

Publication: TUGboat volume 41, number 2 (2020), pages 208–211

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/41-2/tb128ruckert-hint
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Electronic Documents

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: The HINT file format is intended as a replacement of the DVI or PDF file format for on-screen reading of TeX output. We give an overview of the current state of the project and solicit answers to open questions that might influence further development.
