Title: Tagging with LaTeX— Part 1: Author considerations

Summary: real-world examples of LaTeX source to archive accessible PDF.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Ross Moore

Publication: TUGboat volume 41, number 2 (2020), pages 223–242

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/41-2/tb128moore-tagging1
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Electronic Documents

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: Successful tagging within PDF files generated from LaTeX source encourages a change in viewpoint on the nature and intent of the LaTeX coding. Using explicit examples from a real-world document, we illustrate how to capture such a change within the LaTeX source, for various structural elements. Other issues for creating archival and accessible PDFdocuments are discussed.
