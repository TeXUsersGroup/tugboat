Title: A technical contribution to the description of the 16th century fonts of Polish printers

Summary: splitting page images from Polonia Typographica Saeculi Sedecimi into glyphs.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Janusz S. Bień (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 3 (2025), pages 420–426

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-3/tb144bien-typoglyphs
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Humanities

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: Polonia Typographica Saeculi Sedecimi, published between 1968 and 1981, contains 89 font tables. Scans of these tables have been split into images of individual glyphs, which were used to reconstruct the tables in a LuaLaTeX document. The images can also be browsed using the Geeqie program or a similar tool.
