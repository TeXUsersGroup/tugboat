Abstract: The Portuguese TeX community held its first meeting on September 4, 2025, in Évora, Portugal. Colocated with INForum (www.inforum.pt), the event gathered academics and professionals to discuss recent developments in LaTeX, practical documentation workflows, design, typography, and the use of AI tools in document preparation. This report summarizes the program, participation, and future outlook for the Portuguese TeX Users Group (TUG-PT, www.tug.pt).
