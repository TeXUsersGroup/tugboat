Title: Flagging

Summary: flag presentations, QR tags for errors, run status via colored lights.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Hans Hagen

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 3 (2025), pages 404–409

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-3/tb144hagen-flagging
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: ConTeXt

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: In this summary I will describe how one goes from using color clues in a PDF presentation at BachoTeX to an equally colorful signaling feature in ConTeXt processing. The presentation was about flags. For quite a while flags have been used for communication, on sea and on land. Before signals became digital, a system of widespread semaphores on towers and hills were using a system of flags that essentially encoded messages over a distance. Today’s communication technologies that rely on inventions and concepts of those times, like encoding and encryption, owe a debt to this.
