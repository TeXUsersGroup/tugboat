Title: Words of Type, for a multilingual and multicultural typography: wordsoftype.com

Summary: project to translate and explain the vocabulary of typography.

Abstract: Have you had the chance to explain what you do to someone else? Someone who doesn’t know anything about typography, or who doesn’t speak the same language as you? Or—if English is your mother tongue—to someone who is not as fluent? To do any of these, there are many criteria to be taken into consideration if you want a meaningful exchange, here in the context of typography, but in fact, isn’t it the same for everything in life?

First published in January 2025 at the event of Face/Interface conference at Stanford University, Words of Type (wordsoftype.com) brings together the terms used in typography and typeface design in a multilingual and online encyclopedia. Its first version contains 200 terms, illustrated and explained into seven languages, and aims to grow with additional words and languages.

The goals of the project are: first, to bring easier access to typography knowledge; then, to nurture diversity in the field, from the smaller worlds of various expertises related to it, to those of the people speaking the various languages of the larger world, and to make our lives as typographers ever more exciting.
