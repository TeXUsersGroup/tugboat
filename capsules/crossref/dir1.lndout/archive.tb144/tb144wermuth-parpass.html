Title: On differences in paragraph length between TeX's first and second pass

Summary: unexpected results between TeX's paragraph-breaking passes.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 3 (2025), pages 410–419

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-3/tb144wermuth-parpass
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Methods

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: This article looks at examples of straight-text paragraphs that are typeset with a different number of lines in TeX’s first and second pass. Among other results it is shown that the difference is unbounded if the paragraph length can be arbitrarily large.
