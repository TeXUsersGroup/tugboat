Title: A look at complaints about The TeXbook

Summary: analyzing The TeXbook with respect to criticisms over the years.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 3 (2025), pages 381–396

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-3/tb144wermuth-texbook
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Education

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: This article looks at the first volume of Donald E. Knuth’s Computers & Typesetting book series, titled The TeXbook. It does not review this book in the classical sense, but rather lists aspects of this book’s style and contents that people dislike and criticize. Next, the paper discusses them one by one.
