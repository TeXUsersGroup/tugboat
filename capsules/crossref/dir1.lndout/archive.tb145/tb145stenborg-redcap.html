Title: Mathematical notation in REDCap online data collection instruments

Summary: inserting math from LaTeX in online surveys via PDF to SVG.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Travis Stenborg (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 89–90

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145stenborg-redcap
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Electronic Documents

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: Online data collection forms (surveys, questionnaires, etc.) may feature mathematical notation. Several math rendering methods were tested on the REDCap data collection platform. HTML/CSS and text-to-image rendered LaTeX are both viable, and suited to simple and complex math, respectively.
