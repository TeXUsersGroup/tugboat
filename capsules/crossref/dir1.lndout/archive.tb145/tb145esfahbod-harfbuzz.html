Title: HarfBuzz: The quest for universal text shaping

Summary: the evolution of digital text shaping for complex scripts: HarfBuzz, TeX, and Unicode.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Behdad Esfahbod

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 22–27

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145esfahbod-harfbuzz
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Multilingual Document Processing

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: HarfBuzz (github.com/harfbuzz/harfbuzz) is a text shaping engine: it takes Unicode characters and a font and outputs positioned glyphs. It is the industry-standard open-source solution for text shaping, relied upon by billions of users in browsers, operating systems, and word processors—and essential for complex scripts such as Arabic and Indic that require reordering, ligatures, and other transformations.

This article traces the evolution of digital text shaping and the formation of HarfBuzz, the parallel evolution of TeX engines toward Unicode and complex scripts, their integration with HarfBuzz, and a vision for the future of fonts and shaping.
