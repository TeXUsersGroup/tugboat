Title: Baseline grid typesetting with plain TeX

Summary: practical macros to help grid typesetting of plain TeX documents, including math, insertions, etc.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 119–134

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145wermuth-grid
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Methods

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: The article discusses the problem of baseline grid typesetting with plain TeX. It argues that not all structures of TeX should be forced to obey a grid. Macros are presented that allow an author to type straight text on a grid if various grid-leaving primitives and macros are avoided. Macros for mathematics, boxes, and insertions are also developed; their application sometimes requires manual assistance from the author.
