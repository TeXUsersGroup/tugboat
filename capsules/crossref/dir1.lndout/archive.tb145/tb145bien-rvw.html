Title: The use of rv and w in old Polish printed works

Summary: use of `w' vs. `rv' in early printed Polish works.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Janusz S. Bień (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 34–37

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145bien-rvw
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Humanities

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: One of the first printed works in Polish was prepared by the German printer Johann Haller, active in Kraków, who followed the German custom of using the sequence rv (with a round r) for the letter w.

There also existed a distinct letter w, resembling the ligature rv but with a straight r. The Hieronimus font created by Anna Wieluńska covers many characters used in old Polish publications, including this very character.
