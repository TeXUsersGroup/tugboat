Title: Looseness processing costs

Summary: examining \looseness time and memory costs.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Hans Hagen

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 112–113

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145hagen-looseness
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Software & Tools

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: In order to satisfy \looseness, it’s necessary to remember many more solutions, even bad ones. After the whole paragraph is processed, a special looseness loop will try to get fewer or more lines depending on the looseness value. Therefore, passive nodes that normally get cleaned up now stay around.
