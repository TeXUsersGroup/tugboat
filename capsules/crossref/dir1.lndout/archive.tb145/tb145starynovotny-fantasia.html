Title: From footage to film: Recording and editing the Czech premiere of Fantasia Apocalyptica

Summary: processing steps from raw audio and video to the final master, mostly using free and open source tools.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Abstract: On October 11, 2019, the Czech premiere of the multimedia work Fantasia Apocalyptica [8] took place at Masaryk University, marking the 25th anniversary of its Faculty of Informatics (FI MU) and featuring the composer and author Donald Knuth. The performance was recorded and later published on the faculty’s YouTube channel. [4]

In this article, written on the occasion of Knuth’s 88th birthday, we describe the complete preparation of the recording, from processing the raw audio and video footage through recreating the projection panels that accompanied the performance to assembling the final master. With the exception of super-resolution enhancement, introductory and closing edits, and audio compression, the workflow relies exclusively on free and open-source tools, including TeX, Audacity, Aegisub, FFmpeg, MLT, PDFtk, and ImageMagick. The article explains how these tools can be combined into a practical, reproducible workflow that readers may apply to their own recordings.
