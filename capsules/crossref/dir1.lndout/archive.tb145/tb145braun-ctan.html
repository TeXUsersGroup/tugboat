Title: CTAN 2025: Architecture, operation, team and future

Summary: current state of the central infrastructure of the TeX world.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Erik Braun

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 28–33

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145braun-ctan
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Resources

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: The Comprehensive TeX Archive Network (CTAN) is the central infrastructure through which TeX materials are made available for download. This article provides an overview of its origins, the team, the architecture, the mirror network, the upload process, people, and future developments.
