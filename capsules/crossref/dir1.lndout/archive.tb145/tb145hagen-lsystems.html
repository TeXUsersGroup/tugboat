Title: L-systems: Turtle graphics with MetaPost and Lua

Summary: implementing space-filling curves: dragon curves, Sierpiński fractals, many more.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Authors: Hans Hagen, Mikael P. Sundqvist

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 1 (2026), pages 100–111

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-1/tb145hagen-lsystems
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Graphics

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: The feature that we describe here was inspired by The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants by Przemyslaw Prusinkiewicz and Aristid Lindenmayer (archive.org/details/the-algorithmic-beauty-of-plants). It started when Mikael wanted to draw a few Hilbert curves in order to discuss and show the idea behind space-filling curves in his lectures.

It is well known that one can produce these kinds of graphics and other fractal-like and tree-like nice images using so-called Lindenmayer systems, and of course there are many tools available to do so. We did not know of any such tool working in MetaPost, though, and thought it would be fun to explore the techniques.

We started by very simple turtle methods to code macros that generated a path recursively (that first approach will be shown below), then we got some wrappers, but we quickly went to a (still quite simple) mix of Lua and MetaPost. We could actually leave the path generation to Lua completely, but then we don’t get the fun of playing and applying a feature that we added a few years ago: turtle graphics.
