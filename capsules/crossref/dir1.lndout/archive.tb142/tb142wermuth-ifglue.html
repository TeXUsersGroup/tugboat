Title: Comparisons for glue and muglue

Summary: plain TeX macros for conditional tests of (mu)glue.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 1 (2025), pages 147–150

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-1/tb142wermuth-ifglue
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Macros

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: This article develops macros for the original TeX program that behave like Boolean conditionals to compare corresponding components of two glue or two muglue specifications.
