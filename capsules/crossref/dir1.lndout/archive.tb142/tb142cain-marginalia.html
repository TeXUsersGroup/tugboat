Title: marginalia at work: Running heads, float captions, citations, and small figures in the margins

Summary: configurable LuaLaTeX package for automatic adjustments, optional and required marginal elements, etc.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Alan J. Cain (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 1 (2025), pages 49–53

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-1/tb142cain-marginalia
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: LaTeX

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: This paper is an introduction to the LuaLaTeX package marginalia, whose development was driven by the aim of typesetting a book with running heads, citations, and float captions in the margins. It allows the placement of non-floating marginal content anywhere, automatically adjusts positions to prevent content either overlapping or being pushed off the page, and has flexible key–value configuration.
