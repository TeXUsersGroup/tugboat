Title: Externalization with Memoize

Summary: memoization of typesetting, including TikZ, and other results to avoid recompilation.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Sašo Živanović (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 1 (2025), pages 124–131

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-1/tb142zivanovic-memoize
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Macros

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: Memoize is a fast and flexible package for externalization of typesetting results, and for memoization of compilation results in general. By allowing the author to reuse the results of compilation of computationally intensive pieces of the document, it can radically cut down the compilation time of successive compilations.

In this article I introduce externalization and memoization, outline how they are implemented in Memoize, show how to use the package, and shamelessly try to entice package writers to support it in their packages.
