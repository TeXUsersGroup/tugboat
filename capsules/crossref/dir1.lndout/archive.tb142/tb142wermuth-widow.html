Title: Output routines extending \plainoutput

Summary: reporting and automatic prevention of widow lines, and some help for club lines and hyphenations.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 1 (2025), pages 136–144

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-1/tb142wermuth-widow
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Macros

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: This article explains the implementation of three output routines—some with silent output paths—for plain TeX that address the reporting, prevention, or avoidance of widow lines on single pages and spreads. Club (or orphan) lines as well as hyphenated words at a page break are looked at too.
