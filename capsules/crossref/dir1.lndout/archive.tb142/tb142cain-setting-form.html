Title: Experiences in typesetting a complex book: Consistency, graphics, languages, notes

Summary: some generalizable techniques from typesetting the author's book Form & Number.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Alan J. Cain (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 1 (2025), pages 40–48

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-1/tb142cain-setting-form
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: LaTeX

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: This article discusses various aspects of typesetting the author’s book Form & Number: A History of Mathematical Beauty and some of the techniques that were adopted and which have, in certain cases, formed the bases of new packages. Topics discussed include: the creation of two-page spreads showing tilings for part divisions; notes and other content in the margins; ensuring consistency in graphics; font modifications that were necessary because of unforeseen combinations involving accented letters; indexing; running heads; the build process.
