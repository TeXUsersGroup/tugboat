Title: OpBible—a tool for creating study Bibles

Summary: adding notes, maps, other material to multiple biblical texts.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Authors: Šimon Konečný, Petr Olšák

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 1 (2025), pages 12–18

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-1/tb142konecny-opbible
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Humanities

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: A study Bible can be created by taking the complete text of one of the many variations of the Bible translation from the internet and adding notes to this source text. If you want to do so using TeX, and to apply your general notes to more Bible translations at the same time, then you can use the OpBible package [1]. This package was created via intensive collaboration of two authors. The first author, Šimon Konečný, is mainly a theologian and in addition an average TeX user. He brought the main ideas of what to do and he was the first and main tester of the result. He started to create his extensive note apparatus for new study Bibles in the Czech language [2]. The second author, Petr Olšák, is a TeX expert. He implemented the ideas in the TeX package.

Šimon Konečný wrote the first part of this article. He describes the package from the user’s point of view. Petr Olšák added notes on technical background in the second section of this article where some interesting technical aspects are mentioned.
