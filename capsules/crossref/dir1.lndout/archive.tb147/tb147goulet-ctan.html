Title: How to make a good first impression: A CTAN guide

Summary: requirements and requests for authors uploading to CTAN.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Vincent Goulet (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 301–304

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147goulet-ctan
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Resources

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: New packages hit CTAN nearly every day, on average. Few are accepted on the first upload. The reasons vary, but they are almost always addressed in the upload instructions and recommendations. This shorter guide will tell you about the most frequent pitfalls, how to avoid them, and also how to make updates that live up to your good first impression.
