Title: Writing special keys with Libertinus Keyboard Regular

Summary: a font supporting keyboard keys via simple plain-text ligatures.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Michaël P. van de Weerd

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 310–311

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147weerd-lbkeyboard
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Fonts

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: The Libertinus font family is highly versatile and very well suited as an alternative to Computer Modern when typesetting a document with TeX. One of its family members is Libertinus Keyboard Regular, a font that produces glyphs representing keyboard keys. The font uses ligatures to produce special keys like Esc, all of which have been compiled into an overview in this article.
