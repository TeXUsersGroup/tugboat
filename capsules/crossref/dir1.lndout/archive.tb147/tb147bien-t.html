Title: The letter t in old Polish printed works: A preliminary study

Summary: interpreting and encoding variations of `t', with and without diacritics.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Janusz S. Bień (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 352–360

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147bien-t
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Humanities

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: The t glyphs and their variants were extracted from a typoglyphs repository derived from the multivolume work Polonia Typographica Saeculi Sedecimi (published between 1968 and 1981). The shapes of some glyphs are difficult to interpret because the additional component can be read either as a diacritic, an integral part of the glyph shape or as part of a ligature with another character.

Various diacritics used with the letter are also difficult to interpret. In the paper we discuss in detail some of these problems from the perspective of the Unicode encoding and the Junicode font.
