Title: Kazakh language support for TeX

Summary: extensive new hyphenation patterns for Kazakh.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Berik I. Tuleuov (orcid)

Category: Multilingual Documents

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: This article describes the development of hyphenation patterns and language support for Kazakh in TeX. Approximately 106,000 words were extracted from an academic dictionary and processed to correct numerous encoding and typesetting errors. The Unicode-compatible pypatgen utility was then used to generate hyphenation patterns, which were manually revised to account for the peculiarities of Kazakh syllabification. The resulting pattern dictionary is approximately 160 kilobytes in size and supports pdfLaTeX, XeLaTeX, and LuaLaTeX. We also compare the new patterns with an earlier version available from CTAN and demonstrate several cases in which our version produces more accurate hyphenation.
