Title: The TRIP test: Its history, its current role

Summary: the history, impact, and successes and failures of the TRIP test.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 390–399

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147wermuth-trip
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Methods

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: Donald E. Knuth defined the TRIP test to validate his second implementation of TeX in 1982. It is still an important part of his TeX distribution as it must nowadays be used to validate a concrete implementation. This article does not describe the contents of this test in detail. It concentrates on the history and the impact of the TRIP test. The text explains why this test was generated, discusses the test’s successes and failures, and relates the test to the state of software testing at the time of its creation.
