Title: Handling bibliographies and metadata for journal publishers: crossrefware, bibtexperllibs, and TUGboat

Summary: Perl bundles supporting BibTeX and crossref.org uploads, and examples of their usage with TUGboat.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Authors: Karl Berry (orcid), Boris Veytsman (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 328–334

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147berry-crossref
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Electronic Documents

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: This article describes the usage of the crossrefware and bibtexperllibs Perl bundles, currently maintained by the authors, for integrating and uploading article metadata to Crossref (crossref.org). It then gives a case study of how these bundles are used in the TUGboat production process.
