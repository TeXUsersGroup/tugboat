Title: Human or artificial intelligence

Summary: where AI helped with analyzing the ConTeXt system, where it didn't.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Authors: Hans Hagen, Keith McKay, Mikael P. Sundqvist

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 372–376

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147hagen-human-ai
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Artificial Intelligence

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: Let’s start with the observation that what is currently presented as Artificial Intelligence is indeed as artificial as can be. It doesn’t come spontaneously out of silicon fed with lots of energy. It’s essentially the results of human intelligence collected and presented in a specific way. The cornerstone of this technology is Large Language Models (LLMs), where language is interpreted rather liberally, because images are also part of the game and the languages meant for computers are also fed into the machinery.
