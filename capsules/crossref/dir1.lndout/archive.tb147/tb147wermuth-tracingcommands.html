Title: Tracing primitives in the original TeX

Summary: detailed discussion of \tracingcommands, also \tracingmacros and macro debugging.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Udo Wermuth

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 400–413

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147wermuth-tracingcommands
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Methods

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: This article describes two debugging aids of the program TeX. It primarily focuses on the integer parameter \tracingcommands that distinguishes two levels when it writes information about processed built-in commands into TeX’s log file. The article also looks at the integer parameter \tracingmacros, which is a useful partner of \tracingcommands.
