Title: Abstract diagrams for the working mathematician: The Asymptote smoothmanifold module

Summary: producing diagrams in differential geometry, topology, set theory, etc.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Roman Maksimovich

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 3 (2026), pages 380–385

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-3/tb147maksimovich-diagrams
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Graphics

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: Within the niche of mathematicians, there’s a niche of those who need to draw diagrams related to pure mathematics. Within that niche there’s a niche of people who have experience in programming and feel comfortable writing code to produce graphics. This article is written with this niche in mind. It’s a survey of the available tools and a showcase of a library I wrote specifically to simplify the process of drawing diagrams in differential geometry, topology, set theory, etc.
