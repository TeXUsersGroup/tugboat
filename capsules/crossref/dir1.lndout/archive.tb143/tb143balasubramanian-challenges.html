Title: Challenges faced by the publishing industry

Summary: overview of difficulties and solutions in today's technical publishing.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Veeraraghavan Balasubramanian

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 2 (2025), pages 187–189

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-2/tb143balasubramanian-challenges
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Publishing

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: This paper explores the key challenges faced by the publishing industry, especially in the context of LaTeX-based workflows, including manuscript submission complexities, operational hurdles, accessibility issues, supply chain logistics, and technological evolution. It provides insights into industry support for LaTeX, recent trends, and future directions aimed at fostering innovation, collaboration, and inclusivity in scholarly publishing. The discussion emphasizes the critical role of technological advancements such as AI and automation in transforming the landscape.
