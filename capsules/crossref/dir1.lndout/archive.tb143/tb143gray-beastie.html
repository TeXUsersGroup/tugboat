Title: Beastie: A new (and the old) bibliography processor

Summary: a new Scheme bibliography processor, and discussion of longstanding BibTeX issues with ways forward.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Norman Gray (orcid)
