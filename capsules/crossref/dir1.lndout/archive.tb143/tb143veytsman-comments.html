Title: A system for editorial comments

Summary: layered and colored marginalia for author/editor communication.

Full text of article: publicly available now.

Author: Boris Veytsman (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 46, number 2 (2025), pages 189–194

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/46-2/tb143veytsman-comments
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Publishing

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: Copyediting a paper requires a back and forth exchange between the authors and editors. The traditional way to communicate editorial notes to the authors is marginalia. This paper discusses a system for typesetting layered marginal comments.
