Title: The pursuit of professional and accessible tables in LaTeX

Summary: creating accessible tables, utilizing many packages and hand-coding.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Category: Accessibility

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: In academic publishing, typesetting tables remains a significant challenge, requiring a delicate balance between rigorous publisher styling and modern accessibility mandates. The Comprehensive TeX Archive Network (CTAN) provides numerous options, such as booktabs, tabularx, tabulary, longtable, tabularray, and more. Finding a single package among these that offers features like column and cell merging, cell and row colors, page breaking, caption styling, footnotes, etc., remains a persistent challenge. In this article, we will explore the features of prominent packages to determine which tools are most effective for creating professional-grade tables that remain fully accessible to all readers.
