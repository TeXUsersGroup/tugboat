Title: V3D: Portable interactive 3D graphics for PDF and beyond

Summary: improved rendering and portability for 3D vector graphics, with examples using Asymptote.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: John C. Bowman

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 2 (2026), pages 230–233

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-2/tb146bowman-v3d
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Graphics

Difficulty: Advanced

Abstract: The V3D file format provides a portable, compressed representation for interactive 3D vector graphics generated by the TeX-aware vector graphics language Asymptote. It replaces the legacy PRC format, which was limited to Adobe Acrobat and lacked support for transparency, Bézier patches with vertex-dependent colors, and Bézier triangle patches. V3D content can be embedded in PDF files, viewed with an Okular plugin on GNU/Linux, or displayed online through a JavaScript-based viewer. We describe the format’s features, demonstrate its capabilities through examples, and discuss its role in the ongoing modernization of Asymptote’s rendering pipeline.
