Title: Inferring logical structure of a scientific paper from visual representation: An easy task for humans, but surprisingly hard for AI

Summary: results of training LLMs to recover LaTeX structuring.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Authors: Prasad Adhiyaman, Shreyans Babel, Chhandak Bagchi, Boris Veytsman (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 2 (2026), pages 277–280

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-2/tb146adhiyaman-inferring
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Methods

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: We created a set of obfuscated scientific papers in which a logical structure is changed to a visual one, and trained several models to reconstruct the original papers. The results were not very good for out-of-the-box open source LLMs, but improved after fine tuning. The results for the BERT model were also acceptable. The trained models were able to convert the initially visually formatted articles from arXiv to logical formatting, but the quality of the translation needs improvement. We argue that reconstruction of the logical structure requires several reading passes with changing focus.
