Title: Preparing SSHRC Insight Grant applications in LaTeX with sshrc-insight

Summary: developing complex grant proposals, with multilingual support and more.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Author: Tristan Miller (orcid)

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 2 (2026), pages 186–194

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-2/tb146miller-sshrc
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: LaTeX

Difficulty: Intermediate Plus

Abstract: The Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada (SSHRC) requires applications to its Insight Grants program to be split into a multitude of plain-text and PDF documents. This makes it challenging for teams of grant seekers to maintain consistency in formatting and cross-referencing, particularly when using word processors. This article describes the development and use of a LaTeX class for Insight Grants proposals, sshrc-insight.

In addition to formatting the proposal according to SSHRC’s specifications, the class allows authors to easily switch between producing a single, unified PDF for the writing and internal review phase, and multiple PDFs for final submission. Additional features include stable citation numbering across compilation modes, automatic character counts for the plain-text parts, and French localization.

Beyond providing an overview of the class and its intended workflow, this article describes the solutions to various technical challenges that were required to implement its features. The aim is thus not only to introduce a practical tool for grant writers, but also to provide guidance to developers of classes for similar fragmented, portal-driven document workflows.
