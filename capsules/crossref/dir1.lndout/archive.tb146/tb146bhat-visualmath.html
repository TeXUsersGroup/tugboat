Title: Visual impairment meets mathematics: Bypassing institutional inertia

Summary: STEM learning for the visually impaired via code-based workflows, from a student and his coach.

Full text of article: available to TUG members (join TUG); will be publicly available after the next issue is published.

Authors: Aarush Bhat, Timothy Erickson

Publication: TUGboat volume 47, number 2 (2026), pages 195–202

DOI (this page): 10.47397/tb/47-2/tb146bhat-visualmath
(previous doi - next doi)

Category: Accessibility

Difficulty: Intermediate

Abstract: Access to higher education in STEM fields for visually impaired students is often obstructed by institutional inertia and inaccessible visual workflows. This paper, reflecting on our presentation and subsequent learnings at the TeX User Group’s 2026 conference, outlines a code-based approach to circumventing these barriers. By treating visual layouts and spatial mathematics as structured data via (La)TeX, Markdown, and structural OCR, we propose an ecosystem where accessibility is baked into the source code.

While a comprehensive study on these methodologies is currently being authored for the University of Alberta’s Undergraduate Research Initiative (URI), this article serves as an intermediate reflection of our findings, enriched by the invaluable sounding board of the (La)TeX community.
